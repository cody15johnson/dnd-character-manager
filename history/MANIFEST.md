## Provenance and limitations

- These are archived source snapshots, not reconstructed releases.
- The two October 12 snapshots are labeled by their relative save order because their HTML does not identify a formal release version.
- Git commits made during this import reflect the time the files were archived, not the original authoring or release dates.
- Available release notes and test reports are stored in the sibling `release-notes/` and `tests/` directories.
- No unsupported historical version numbers or artificial commit dates have been introduced.
